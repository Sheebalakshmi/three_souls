A NOTE FOR YOU BOTH

To my favorite people,

I wish I could freeze a few moments. Not the big ones. Just the ordinary ones — sitting together, taking random photos, laughing at something stupid, complaining about college, and knowing that tomorrow we would meet again.

Because soon, tomorrow won't look like this anymore.

And honestly? I already know I'm going to miss us.

But this isn't a goodbye page. This is a promise. A reminder that before life takes us in different directions, three people shared one beautiful chapter.

No matter where we go, please remember this: you will always have a place in my life that nobody else can replace.
